Your phone is the putter

  1. Hold the phone upright in both hands like a putter grip, arms hanging, screen facing the hole.
  2. Tap Putt and hold still until the beep.
  3. Make a smooth pendulum stroke. Stroke speed sets distance. Twisting the phone opens or closes the face.

Swipe the green to aim. No sensor? Drag back from the ball and release.
